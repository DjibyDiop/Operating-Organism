# BOT MANIFESTO: Constitution and Peripheral Sovereignty

> **FUNDAMENTAL LAW**
> A Bot (e.g., NBIA) is a local peripheral organism. Although it possesses a survival instinct (Local Sovereignty), the **global Constitution of the Organism (oo-constitution)** is above it and possesses the absolute right of decision over it. OPI (the brain) proposes, but the OO Constitution ultimately disposes.

This manifesto defines the architecture and rules of engagement for all peripheral organisms (Bots) in the OO ecosystem.

## 1. What is a Bot?
A Bot is the peripheral nervous system (Sensori-Motor) of the OO colony. Unlike OPI (the cognitive brain), the Bot interacts with the physical world or low-level interfaces. It combines the hyper-reactivity of a rule-based system (reflexes in D+) with the cryptographic security of a bare-metal organism.

## 2. Who holds its authority?
**Supreme Authority belongs to `oo-constitution`.**
Normally, the Bot obeys decrees signed by the Global Constitution of the Organism. The Bot's Local Authority only comes into play for immediate physical emergency reflexes or if it finds itself isolated from the rest of the system (Split-Brain).

## 3. What can it receive from OPI?
Via the Hermes network, the Bot receives *Proposals*:
- OPI cannot write directly to the Bot's memory.
- OPI proposes, the Bot disposes.

## 4. What can OPI never impose on it?
OPI (the intelligence) can never bypass the Bot's physical limits, because **only the global Constitution (oo-constitution)** holds the authority to redefine security rules. OPI cannot force a vital action if `oo-constitution` or the local Warden (as a last resort) opposes it.

## 5. Who can modify its Constitution?
Modifying the Bot's vital rules is a sovereign act dictated by the **global Constitution (oo-constitution)** via a highly secure network "Meiotic Splicing", or by a local hardware emergency key in case of total compromise.

## 6. What triggers its instinct?
Environmental and hardware sensors connected to the Bot (the *Sensor Bus*).
When a sensor crosses a critical threshold, the instinct layer (D+ virtual machine) preempts any ongoing communication with OPI to execute an immediate defensive reflex.

## 7. How does it communicate?
Via the **Hermes Mesh** (UDP/UART).
- **Zéro Mocks Policy**: The Bot uses a real transport (Shared Memory, UDP, or physical UART). No stubs or simulations are permitted for vital communications.
- It only broadcasts "Sensory Facts" and receives "Proposals".

## 8. What happens during a split-brain?
If the connection with OPI or the colony is severed, the Bot enters **ISOLATED SURVIVAL MODE**.
It executes its D+ reflexes in a loop to maintain physical homeostasis until communication is restored.

## 9. Is the Bot intelligent?
No. The Bot is **reactive**. It does not think, it feels and acts. Intelligence is the exclusive domain of OPI. The Bot is an organ, not the brain.

## 10. The Warden's Guarantee
Every action requested by OPI or triggered by the D+ reflexes MUST pass through the local Warden before reaching the physical actuators. The Warden verifies that the action does not violate the Bot's physical integrity limits.
